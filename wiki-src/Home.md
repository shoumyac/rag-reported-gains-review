# RAG reported-gains review: wiki

**Question:** Do reported gains in retrieval-augmented generation survive scrutiny?

**Registration:** https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/XGPCZ

## Pages

- Protocol overview: see `protocol/` in the repository and the OSF registration.
- Search log: dates, counts and hashes of each search run (added after the search freeze).
- Screening log: stage counts and agreement statistics (added during screening).
- Extraction codebook: `protocol/extraction_instructions.md`.
- Claim stress test: configurations, models and judges used (added when run).
- Decisions and deviations: `protocol/deviations_log.md`.

## Who does what

- Anmita Das (first author) and Shoumya Chowdhury (second author) design, run and check the review.
- Independent annotators, who are students in Bangladesh and are acknowledged with their consent, screen and verify the authors' own papers and settle disagreements.
- Language models are used as documented assistants for ranking, extraction and experiments. They never make a decision alone.

Protocol registered 1 November 2026. Search not yet run.
